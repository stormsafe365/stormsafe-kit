Which to Choose

Certified vs Non-Certified

Florida and coastal/wind-prone zones require certified. If you're in a low-wind, no-permit area, non-certified can save 15–25%.

Certified
Engineered & stamped
  • Engineered for specific wind & snow loads to local code
  • Required in Florida and all coastal / hurricane zones
  • Stronger anchoring, heavier-gauge framing, sealed connections
  • Comes with stamped engineering drawings for permitting
  • Eligible for lifetime structural warranty
Best for: FL · GA · LA · TX · NC · SC
Non-Certified
Reliable & affordable
  • Best for areas with no permit / no inspection requirements
  • Same materials, lighter anchor & bracing spec
  • Saves 15–25% vs certified equivalent
  • Not engineered for hurricane winds or heavy snow loads
  • 20-year rust-through warranty still applies
Best for: Inland, rural, no-code zones
