What Changes As You Go Wider

Three Things To Know

01

Every frame carries the truss

The drawings show the end wall, but the same truss repeats on every frame down the length of your building. A 40' × 60' workshop isn't two braced ends with plain rafters between them.

02

Legs scale with the span

Up to 30' wide, single posts. From 31' to 51', double posts. At 52' and up, laced ladder legs. Wider roof, more load into the ground, heavier column to take it.

03

Certified drawings govern

These illustrations show the style of frame for each width. Your stamped engineering set, sized for your county's wind zone, is what actually gets built and permitted.
